About that door

My books are sweet with real heat. You'll get slow burns, almost-kisses that make you yell at the page, and actual kisses that were worth the wait. You'll get men who look at the heroine like she personally invented summer.

When things go further, the door closes. You know what happened. I know what happened. We don't need to watch.

Hi, I'm Emma

I grew up on the movies where somebody runs through an airport in the rain and the whole town shows up for the big gesture. I still think those are the best stories there are, so now I write them. Mine are set in places with one stoplight, a diner that's been open since 1962, and a guy who's been waiting a very long time for you to notice him.

My heroes are the patient kind. They fix your porch step without being asked and remember how you take your coffee. They will wait. They will also, eventually, lose their minds about it a little, and that's my favorite part to write.

When I'm not writing, I'm probably at the beach with a paperback and a sandwich that has too much sand in it to finish.

xo, Emma
